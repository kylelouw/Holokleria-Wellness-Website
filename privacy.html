Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This page is a practical draft based on the website as it currently operates. Confirm the business identity, contact details, retention practices, and applicable legal requirements before publishing it as your final policy.

Who is responsible for your information?

Holokleria Wellness is responsible for personal information you choose to share through this website or directly with the business. Before publication, add the business’s legal or trading name and a monitored privacy contact address to this page.

What information may you provide?

The contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, the topic of your enquiry, and your message. Please avoid including sensitive health information in a general enquiry. If you contact the business by phone or email, the information you provide in that conversation may also be used to respond.

Why is it used?

Information you provide is used to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss a product or service with you. The website currently does not provide online checkout or appointment booking. Do not use the contact form for emergencies or urgent medical concerns.

How is information handled and shared?

The contact form is configured to open an email application; it is not connected to a website database. If you send an email, that message will be handled by the email services involved in sending and receiving it. The website loads web fonts from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash; those providers may receive technical request information when their resources are requested. Confirm any other hosting, analytics, or third-party services in use before launch and update this notice accordingly.

How long is information kept?

Information should be kept only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and meet applicable record-keeping obligations, then securely deleted or de-identified. The business should set and follow an appropriate retention schedule for messages it receives.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to ask to access, correct, or delete personal information, or object to certain uses. To make a request, contact the business using the details on the contact page. If you are in South Africa, you may also have rights under POPIA and may contact the Information Regulator where appropriate.

Updates to this policy

This notice may be updated when the website or the way enquiries are handled changes. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
